# Modern KPI Integration Demo Guide

## Overview
This demo showcases how modern KPIs can be integrated with traditional business metrics to create a comprehensive view of business performance and revenue impact.

## Key Features

### 1. Executive Dashboard
- **Real-time KPI monitoring** with live data simulation
- **Revenue impact visualization** showing direct business correlation
- **Multi-dimensional trend analysis** combining operational and financial metrics
- **Predictive insights** demonstrating proactive management capabilities

### 2. Traditional vs Modern KPI Comparison
- **Side-by-side comparison** of traditional reactive metrics vs modern predictive KPIs
- **Performance improvement visualization** showing 20-40% gains across key areas
- **Methodology differences** highlighting automation and correlation benefits

### 3. KPI Correlation Analysis
- **Interactive correlation matrix** showing how KPIs interconnect
- **Business impact amplification** through reinforcing metric cycles
- **Cross-functional dependencies** revealing hidden relationships

### 4. Revenue Impact Flow
- **Sankey diagram** mapping KPIs to revenue stages
- **Financial priority analysis** by revenue stage
- **Cash flow acceleration** through operational improvements

### 5. Business Unit Views
- **Department-specific dashboards** for Infrastructure, AppOps, Delivery, Help Desk
- **Role-based KPI focus** aligned with team responsibilities
- **Revenue connection clarity** for each operational area

### 6. Data Sources Mapping
- **200+ data source integration** visualization
- **KPI category distribution** across operational domains
- **Source prioritization** by KPI impact and count

## Running the Demo

```bash
# Start the demo
python start_demo.py

# Or run directly
streamlit run enhanced_demo.py
```

The demo will open in your browser at http://localhost:8501

## Data Sources
- `data_sources_to_kpis_mapping.csv` - Maps 200+ sources to specific KPIs
- `kpi_outcome_mapping.csv` - Links KPIs to business outcomes
- `kpi_revenue_cashflow_mapping.csv` - Shows financial impact by revenue stage

## Customization
The demo can be customized by:
- Updating CSV files with your specific data sources and KPIs
- Modifying the correlation matrix based on your business relationships
- Adjusting revenue impact calculations for your industry
- Adding new visualization pages for specific use cases
